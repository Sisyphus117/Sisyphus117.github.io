问题来源于Hands on Machine Learning第二版

题目和解答

  1. What is the fundamental idea behind Support Vector Machines?
    1. 类别是线性可分的
    2. 用一条线(decision boundary)将各类别隔开
  2. What is a support vector?
    1. 大部分样本是”off the street”的
    2. 决策边界是被边缘的实例确定的
    3. 这些实例被称为支持向量
  3. Why is it important to scale the inputs when using SVMs?
    1. SVM是特征规模敏感的,如图
    2. 旨在通过特征缩放得到更好的决策边界
  4. Can an SVM classifier output a confidence score when it classifies an instance? What about a probability?
    1. 可以得到信心得分,但不能得到概率
  5. Should you use the primal or the dual form of the SVM problem to train a model on a training set with millions of instances and hundreds of features?
    1. 应当使用对偶问题(dual form), 因为他简化了计算流程(by using kernel trick)
    2. 对于n远大于m的问题效果更显著
  6. Say you trained an SVM classifier with an RBF kernel. It seems to underfit the training set: should you increase or decrease γ (gamma)? What about C?
    1. γ和C的大小影响实例的影响范围,γ和C越大,决策边界越平滑
    2. 当过拟合,减小γ和C,反之增大这两个超参数
